It's finally out. Ever since it came out in 2013, I've had people ask for an English translation. I just couldn't do it, because when I start translating my own work I start rewriting everything. I gave up. Until I didn't have to do this myself.

In 2013 Humanist forlag published my autobiography, "Nittenåttifire". Twenty-five years in Scientology. To the top of the Bridge, OT 8, and out again. The benefits, the innermost secrets, the abuse of power. Now it is in English. Nineteen Eighty-Four. 314 pages. Free.

**[Download the PDF](/assets/files/Nineteen-Eighty-Four-Geir-Isene.pdf)**, or visit [the book page](/1984).

## The translation

AI did the translation. Then every chapter was checked against the Norwegian. English was fixed where it tripped. The voice is still mine: short sentences, straight talk.

## The title

I became a Scientologist in 1984. Orwell's book of the same name is no coincidence.
