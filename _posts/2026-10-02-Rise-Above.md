My favorite book is [*Jonathan Livingston Seagull*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Livingston_Seagull) by Richard Bach. It's a deep eye-opener that I have also employed in coaching others.

Rise Above is out. I have been working on this for 5 months. Ten tracks, 36 minutes... my tribute to the book but also spanning classical music, spacesynth, electronica and a touch of psytrance.

On [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/4l43Fkni3Cu2NR1yC6Ge49) and most other streaming services.
