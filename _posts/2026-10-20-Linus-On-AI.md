The [Luddism](https://isene.org/2026/09/The-Identity-Shock.html) is getting crazy.

I follow plenty of forums and subreddits, many of them on AI. Western society was polarized before, and the recent heated discussions on AI become yet another symptom of what ails us.

What that ailing is can be discovered by exploring [TEG](https://isene.com/freewill) and is not the subject of this post. The sentiment of [Linus Torvalds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linus_Torvalds) is. He is the creator and maintainer of [Linux](https://kernel.org).

I have been a Linux user since 1999 and have followed his journey closely. When AI came to the fore, I watched [his take on it evolve](https://itsfoss.com/news/linus-torvalds-on-ai/) until it settled on [this](https://lkml.iu.edu/hypermail/linux/kernel/2610.1/13255.html):

[![Linus Torvalds on the kernel mailing list, 13 October 2026: rules that ban tools are utterly stupid](/assets/posts/linus-on-ai.jpg)](https://lkml.iu.edu/hypermail/linux/kernel/2610.1/13255.html)

I'm with Linus on this. AI is a tool one can embrace for efficiency or oppose to retain inefficiency. As long as you understand the tool and the output, the tool of choice is less significant.
