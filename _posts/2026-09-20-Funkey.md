Espen's challenge paid off. With a weekend I had some spare time to create Funkey.

{% include youtube.html id='yI3RKwR6eDY' %}

Seven games on the [funkey](https://github.com/isene/funkey) engine: climb, jumpman, invaders, drive, Doom, soar and a walk to a castle. The words in the intro are mine. The voice is not. Background music is mine and can be found on Spotify.

## The weekend

Friday: a challenge from Espen: "Make a game engine for the terminal". Saturday: the engine, real pixels through [glass](https://github.com/isene/chasm), then [Doom](https://isene.org/2026/09/Doom.html). The night: sound, sprites, a 3D rasterizer, jumpman, invaders, a drive over the hills and a flight over fractal mountains. Sunday morning: a game picker with screenshots, a textured renderer on all sixteen cores, the castle, and this film.

The castle came last. Stone, slate and wood, a moat, banners in the wind, torches at the gate, crows over the keep. Twenty milliseconds per frame.

![The poster: the castle, with the other games around it](/assets/posts/funkey-poster.png)
