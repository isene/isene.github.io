## The Game Room

Twelve games in your browser. Pick one and play.

- Shooters, arcade classics, puzzles, a kart race, a dungeon, a world of blocks and a wargame.
- Search, or pick a game type: Arcade, Shooting, Thinking, Adventure, Racing.
- A top ten under every game, shared by everyone who plays.
- Comments under every game. No account, no login. Write your name and what you think. It shows at once.
- Your progress stays in your browser for the next visit.

The twelve games in the room: [raid](https://isene.com/games/play.html?g=raid), [kart](https://isene.com/games/play.html?g=kart), [salvo](https://isene.com/games/play.html?g=salvo), [wargame](https://isene.com/games/play.html?g=wargame), [stack](https://isene.com/games/play.html?g=stack), [again](https://isene.com/games/play.html?g=again), [marble](https://isene.com/games/play.html?g=marble), [chomp](https://isene.com/games/play.html?g=chomp), [eliminator](https://isene.com/games/play.html?g=eliminator), [vector](https://isene.com/games/play.html?g=vector), [blocks](https://isene.com/games/play.html?g=blocks) and [jumpman](https://isene.com/games/play.html?g=jumpman).

No sign-up, no ads, straight to the game.

## Wargame

The newest game. There were plenty of hex wargames back in the 80s, and I miss playing them.

Back then the [Aniara](https://no.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aniara_(forening)) gathering at the house of [Johannes Berg](https://no.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_H._Berg_jr.) in Tuengen Allé was a real highlight. Geeks gathered, watched the latest sci-fi, fantasy and horror movies, showed off the latest in computers and played board games. Lots of board games, from [Civilization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civilization_(1980_board_game)) to [Circus Maximus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circus_Maximus_(game)), [Diplomacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplomacy_(game)) and the many strategy war games like "[Drive on Stalingrad](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/5480/drive-on-stalingrad-battle-for-southern-russia-gam)". This is a tribute to those fun times.

Click the game, then `Enter`. On a phone the keys show under it.

- Two scenarios of fourteen turns each.
- North Africa 1942: Rommel's drive from the [Gazala line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Gazala) for Tobruk and the Nile.
- [Stalingrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Stalingrad) 1942: the drive for the Volga, and the Soviet answer in the snow.
- `Left` and `Right` on the title pick the scenario.
- The arrows move the cursor. `Enter` picks a unit and moves it. `Tab` jumps to the next unit that can still act.
- To attack, `Enter` on an enemy. Your units next to it join. `Enter` on one of them leaves it out. `Enter` on the enemy again rolls the die.
- Cut the enemy off from supply. Hold the cities with points when the last turn ends.
- `N` ends the phase. `?` shows the rules. `Q` saves the war.
- Play either side against the computer, two players at one keyboard, or watch the computer play itself.
- The computer's units are one stronger against you. A game against the computer ends with a score.
