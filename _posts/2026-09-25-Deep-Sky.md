When I was 8 and my mother pointed to the three stars making the Belt of Orion I was hooked. I still am, with my telescopes and gear and also with the software I make.

[astro](https://github.com/isene/astro) now shows the deep sky. All 110 Messier objects and all 109 Caldwell objects, at their true size and tilt. The colour shows the type: galaxy, cluster, nebula.

`/` goes to any of them. `M31`, `NGC 7000`, `ring nebula`.

## My eyepieces on the sky

![M31 in four eyepieces](/assets/posts/astro-m31.png)

In Gear mode, pick a telescope and an eyepiece and press `f`. In the sky chart, `v` draws each pair as a circle the size of what it shows.

Four circles on M31. None of the combinations shows the whole galaxy. The 50mm in the Esprit comes closest.

## The night

![The plan for tonight](/assets/posts/astro-plan.png)

`a` adds the object under the crosshair to tonight's plan. `p` shows when each target stands highest, and which pair frames it best.

At the eyepiece, `o` marks it seen, with a note. The night lands in a HyperList file.
