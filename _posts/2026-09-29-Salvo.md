Another game I was pretty good at was Gradius, Konami's 1985 arcade shooter. Hours spent on that game at RA4 game pub down-town Oslo.

After [Crystal Castles](/2026/09/Gems.html) this morning, I had to have that one too.

## The game

- Seven stages: a volcano, stone heads, crystals, living cells, the sun, a high-speed maze and the fortress.
- A boss at the end of each. Three core ships, a stone head, an eye, a fire serpent, and a brain behind walls that grow back.
- Shoot a whole formation, or a red carrier, for a capsule. Each capsule lights the next power-up on the bar.
- `Z` or `Enter` takes what is lit: speed, missiles, double, laser, option, shield.
- Up to four options follow your path and fire with you.
- Every seventh capsule is blue. It clears the screen.
- After the seventh stage it all begins again. Harder.

![The title screen](/assets/posts/salvo-title.png)

## A tribute

A copy of the arcade game would need Konami's own program. So everything here is new: the stages, the ships, the music.

Rock, fire, cells and steel. A frame takes under three milliseconds. glass shows the pixels.
