Click the game, then `Enter`. On a phone the keys show under it.

## The game

- A life is a handful of steps. Then you reincarnate and start over.
- The self you just were walks beside you and presses the same keys.
- Stand on a plate and the door of its colour opens. Step off and it shuts.
- A past self that meets a shut door loses that step and walks on. Keep the door open for it.
- A pale gate lets only a past self through. A bright gate lets only you.
- Put a self on every ring at once and the room is solved.
- The arrows step. `Space` waits a step. `Enter` lets the rest of the life run out.
- `U` takes back a step, `Backspace` the life, `R` the room. `N` and `P` change rooms.
- The music is a round. Each self adds a voice, two bars late.

![Time starts over in a room with four rings](/assets/posts/again-rewind.png)

## The rooms

Twenty of them. The first has one door and two of you. One has four doors and four of you.

A search plays every room. It proves that the room can be solved with the selves and steps it gives. And that it cannot be solved with one self fewer.

The game in this page is the same Rust code as the one in the terminal, built for the browser. 180 KB. It stops when you scroll past it.

![Three selves, two gates and a row of arrows for each life](/assets/posts/again-play.png)
