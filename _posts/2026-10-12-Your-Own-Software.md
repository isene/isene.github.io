I meet a lot of AI resistance. "AI can't be trusted with code." "AI makes lots of mistakes." "AI slop."

There are legitimate reasons to be sceptical or anti-AI. Bad output is not one of them. Let me show you.

{% include youtube.html id='hfFEfyATYVo' %}

The voice is mine. So is the music: Solitude, from [Rise Above](/2026/10/Rise-Above.html). Claude made the rest.

## What it shows

- [funkey](https://isene.org/funkey/): a game engine for the terminal, and its games. It plays the original Doom. Most of the games play in a browser.
- [CHasm](https://isene.org/chasm/): a complete desktop in assembly. Under one megabyte. More than 20 hours of battery life on my laptop.
- [Fe₂O₃](https://isene.org/fe2o3/): more than forty apps in Rust. Mail, calendar, editor, file manager, trading, web browser. The periodic table, the stars, the Standard Model, the universe.
- [ferrite](https://isene.org/ferrite/): an SQL database. One afternoon.
- [nomad](https://isene.org/nomad/): it all follows me onto the phone.
- Several complex web solutions, from a learning platform to a data lake for financial data.

Every application I see is my application. I stopped using other people's applications.

## The film

No video editor. Claude wrote one script that draws every picture of the film.

I read the voice-over in ten takes while the film played. Claude found the time of every word I said and hooked it all up.
