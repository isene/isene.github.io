My autobiography. Published in Norwegian as "Nittenåttifire" by Humanist forlag in 2013. This is the English edition, translated from the Norwegian original.

This book is about Geir Isene, who became a Scientologist in 1984 and who, 25 years later, created a sensation when he left the Church of Scientology. He was and still is the only Scientologist in Norway who has achieved the highest spiritual level, OT 8, and he is the only one who has reached the very top who is writing a book about his experiences. He tells about the benefits he received, about the innermost secrets of this closed organization, and about the abuse of power within the church – an abuse of power that brings to mind the book 1984 by George Orwell. This book will give you a foundation to make better choices when you have to deal with groups and organizations in the future, because you can more easily detect the seeds of abuse of power.
